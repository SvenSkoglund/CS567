Can a visual cue make virtual weight matter?

The problem

A VR controller weighs the same whatever it represents, so the sensation behind many physics lessons is missing.

Research question

Does a pseudo-haptic weight cue, added to direct manipulation in VR, change how accurately learners predict outcomes that depend on mass, density and load?
